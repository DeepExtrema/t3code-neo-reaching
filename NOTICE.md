# Notices

Neo Reaching is put together from two other themes. `build.mjs` reads their files in `vendor/` and writes `theme.json`.

## The picture of the hands

The hands and the wave are the Reaching scene from [neverblinks/backdrops](https://github.com/neverblinks/backdrops),
commit `365998e`. They reach this repository through the Reaching theme for T3 Code
(`DeepExtrema/t3code-reaching`, commit `db87033`), whose `theme.json` is copied unchanged to `vendor/reaching/theme.json`.

Upstream's terms are copied unchanged beside it:

- `vendor/reaching/LICENSE`: the MIT License, for upstream's code. Copyright (c) 2026 neverblinks.
- `vendor/reaching/ARTWORK.md`: the terms for the pictures, which the MIT licence does not cover.

From `ARTWORK.md`, for Reaching:

- The hands are @samdape's dot rendering of the hands in Michelangelo's *Creation of Adam*, used upstream with
  permission. All rights stay with the artist. The fresco itself is public domain.
- The forearms past the artwork's left and right edges are upstream's extension, traced from the fresco.
- Anyone may install and use the theme for free, for any purpose, and may share links to upstream's repository.
- Without permission, nobody may sell the pictures or anything made from them, redistribute them outside
  upstream's repository, claim them as their own, or remove or hide the credits.

`vendor/reaching/theme.json` and `theme.json` both contain the picture. This repository is private for that reason.
Before you make it public or pass `theme.json` on, ask neverblinks, and ask @samdape for the hands.
